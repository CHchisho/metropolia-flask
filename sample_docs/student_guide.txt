Student Guide 2026-2027 - Example University of Applied Sciences (fictional sample document)

1. Studying basics

A bachelor's degree consists of 210 or 240 ECTS credits, depending on the programme. One academic year corresponds to 60 ECTS credits, and one credit represents about 27 hours of student work. The nominal duration of a 240-credit degree is four years.

Every student draws up a personal study plan (PSP) during the first semester. The PSP lists the courses you intend to complete and when. You should review it with your study counsellor at least once a year and update it whenever your plans change.

2. Enrolment and registration

New students must confirm their study place by the deadline stated in the admission letter. At the start of every academic year, all students must register as either attending or non-attending. Registration opens on 1 June and closes on 31 August for the autumn term. Students who do not register lose their right to study until they complete a late registration, which costs 50 euros.

3. Course enrolment

Enrolment to courses opens six weeks before the start of each period and closes one week before the course begins. If a course is full, you are placed on a waiting list and notified by email when a seat becomes available. You can cancel a course enrolment without penalty until the start of the first lesson. After that, a cancellation requires approval from the teacher.

4. Attendance

Attendance rules are defined in the course description. Practical training, laboratory work and group projects usually require at least 80 percent attendance. Lectures are normally not mandatory unless stated otherwise. If you are ill, notify the teacher as soon as possible.

5. Leave of absence

You may apply for a leave of absence (non-attending status) for up to one academic year at a time. Valid reasons include military or civilian service, illness, parental leave and work placements abroad. Apply through the student services portal before the registration deadline. During a leave of absence you cannot enrol in courses or take exams, except for retaking an exam you have already attended.

6. Study counselling

Study counsellors help with study plans, delays in studies, transfers between programmes, and recognition of prior learning. Appointments can be booked online. Drop-in counselling is available on Tuesdays and Thursdays from 12:00 to 14:00 at the student services desk on the first floor.

7. Recognition of prior learning

You can apply for credit transfer for studies completed at another institution or for skills gained through work experience. Submit an official transcript or documentation of your competence to your study counsellor. Decisions are normally made within four weeks.

8. Graduation

To graduate you must complete all required credits, including the bachelor's thesis and practical training, and submit a graduation application. Degree certificates are issued three times a year: in March, June and December.
