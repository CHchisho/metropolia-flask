Student Wellbeing and Support - Example University of Applied Sciences (fictional sample document)

Health care

Degree students are entitled to student health care services. The student health service provides general medical care, mental health services, sexual health advice and vaccinations. Book an appointment by phone between 8:00 and 9:30 on weekdays or through the online booking service. In an emergency, call 112. Dental care is not covered by the student health service.

Psychological support

The university offers free short-term counselling to all students. You can book up to six sessions per academic year with a university psychologist. If you need longer support, the psychologist will help you find the right service. Contact the wellbeing team by email if you feel overwhelmed by studies, stress or personal difficulties. Everything you discuss is confidential.

A 24/7 crisis helpline is available for everyone in acute distress. The number is printed on the back of your student card.

Accessibility

The university aims to make studying possible for everyone. If you have a disability, long-term illness or learning difficulty such as dyslexia, you can ask for support. Support may include extra time in exams, alternative formats of study material, accessible workspaces, or a personal study plan. Contact your study counsellor or the accessibility coordinator to discuss the options. Please make requests as early as possible so arrangements can be made in time.

Financial support

Students may apply for study grants and housing supplements from the national social insurance institution. The university's financial aid advisor can explain the process and help with the application. A small emergency fund exists for students facing sudden financial hardship; apply with a short written explanation to student services.

Student union

The student union represents students in university decision-making and organises events, orientation weeks and peer tutoring. Membership is voluntary and costs 110 euros for the whole degree. Members receive a student card with discounts on public transport and meals. The union office is located in the main building, room A104.

Student restaurants

Subsidised student meals cost 2.95 euros for students with a valid student card. The restaurant on the ground floor is open on weekdays from 10:30 to 14:00. There are vegetarian and lactose-free options every day.

Equality and non-discrimination

Discrimination, harassment and bullying are not tolerated. If you experience or witness inappropriate behaviour, report it to the equality contact person or to student services. Reports can be made anonymously through the online form. Every report is taken seriously and handled confidentially.
